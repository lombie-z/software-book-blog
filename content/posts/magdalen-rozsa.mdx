## Why I made it

Magdalen is my mum, and she paints. I built this for her for Mother's Day, so she'd have somewhere of her own to show her work.

## The rose

The home page is a rose vine, drawn in three.js, that grows up from the bottom of the screen. It grows differently every visit: each new stretch of stem leans a random amount, branches split off by chance, and leaves and roses land wherever the branches happen to go. Older growth slowly fades out, so the vine keeps renewing itself, and a watercolour pass over the top softens it towards her paintings.

The idea came from the growing plant on [Philip Schöningh's site](https://philip-schoeningh.de/).

## Keeping it simple

There's very little interface, because it doesn't need any: her name, a link through to her watercolours, and a way to find out about her or get in touch.

## Editing

Mum updates the site herself through TinaCMS, changing text and adding new pieces in Tina's visual editor without going near the code. What I'd really like is an MCP server, so she could ask an AI assistant to make changes for her, but she doesn't have a subscription that would support that yet. That's the next thing.
