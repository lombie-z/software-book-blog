## Why I made it

Magdalen is my mum, and she paints. I built this for her for Mother's Day, so she'd have somewhere of her own to show her work.

## The rose

The home page is a rose vine, drawn in three.js, growing up from the bottom of the screen. I randomised how far each new stretch of stem leans and where the branches split, so you get a different vine on each visit. Older growth fades away as new growth comes in, and a watercolour pass over the top brings it closer to her paintings.

I took the idea from the growing plant on [Philip Schöningh's site](https://philip-schoeningh.de/).

## Keeping it simple

The site has almost no interface. You see her name and a link to her watercolours, with her contact details one click away.

## Editing

Mum updates the site herself in TinaCMS, changing text and adding new pieces in the visual editor without touching code. Next I want to build an MCP server so she can ask an AI assistant to make changes for her, once she has a subscription that supports it.
