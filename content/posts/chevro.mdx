## How to play

Two chevrons fly out of the middle in opposite directions. Tap the mirrors and pipes to rotate them and steer the chevrons back into each other. When they collide, the level's done. You can keep turning a block right up until a chevron reaches it.

Later levels add item cubes to place on the board and animals that blast a whole row or column, and there's a daily puzzle too.
