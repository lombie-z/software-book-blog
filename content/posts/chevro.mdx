## How to play

Two chevrons fly out of the middle in opposite directions. Tap the mirrors and pipes to rotate them and steer the chevrons back into each other. The level ends when they collide. You can keep turning a block until a chevron reaches it.

Later levels give you item cubes to place on the board and animals that blast a whole row or column. There's also a daily puzzle.
