## From the planter to the pond

FogDrog starts where the micro biome on my home page leaves off: the same clay planter, with a mossy hand rising out of the water. Scroll, and the screen breaks into pixels, the camera tips over the rim, follows the water down the falls and settles at a pond at sunset.

## The pond

At the bottom there's a frog on a lily pad, a golden retriever wading about, and an old road sign with the name on it. The dog walks a while, stops to sniff the water, wanders off behind the reeds and comes back up close for a look. The only buttons hang under the sign, at its angle.

## Modelled in Blender, with Claude

Everything in the scene is built in Blender from scripts Claude wrote and ran: the hand, the signs, the dog. The dog went through rounds against photos of real retrievers and Muybridge's 1887 plates of a walking dog, so its paws stay planted as it walks. Its coat is shell fur, drawn in the browser.

## The game

FogDrog is about a dog and a frog trying to get by in a world after people: an exploration mode, and an idle mode that keeps growing. The site is its front door for now.
